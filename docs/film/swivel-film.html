Can a window glide back after I drag it?
Yes. Swivel waits until you let go, then glides it back into its slot in the 3-way split, at its normal size.
And if I resize the middle one?
That width becomes the new middle. The side windows narrow or widen to make room, and each screen remembers its own.
Leave full screen alone, though.
It does. A full-screen window stays put until you shrink it, then it drops back into place.

Swivel

A menu-bar app for ultrawide displays. The window you focus sits in the middle with a soft glow, and everything else tiles on either side.

How it behaves

  • Click a side window and it swivels to the middle.
  • Drag inside a side window and it stays on its side.
  • Resize a side window and it keeps your size.
  • Drag a title bar and it glides back into place.
  • Resize the middle and that becomes the middle width.
  • Full screen stays full screen until you shrink it.

Swivel, in six moves

On an ultrawide screen, the window you focus sits in the middle with a blue glow. Everything else tiles on either side.

  • You doSwivel does
  • Click a side windowIt swivels into the middle and takes the glow. Only those two windows move.
  • NewDrag inside a side windowReorder a tab, select text, drag a fileIt takes the glow but stays on its side. A plain click still brings it over.
  • NewResize a side windowIt keeps the size you gave it. Nothing else moves.
  • NewDrag a window by its title barWhen you let go, it glides back into its place. A resized side window returns to its normal size, too.
  • NewResize the middle windowThat becomes the middle width. It re-centers, the sides make room, and each screen remembers its own.
  • NewMake a window full screenDouble-click the title bar, or use the green buttonIt stays full screen and nothing else moves. Shrink it, and it drops back into its place.

Shortcuts

  • ⌃⌥⌘TTurn Swivel off or on
  • ⌃⌥⌘FLeave one window alone
  • ⌃⌥⌘[/]Bring the left or right window to the middle
  • Menu bar icon → Re-tile resets everything
